# OVARY-X — Data Sources and Limitations

## 1. Project objective

OVARY-X is a research proof-of-concept for a personalized digital twin that combines longitudinal health-profile information with time-series signals to demonstrate data integration, baseline tracking, state estimation, and scenario simulation.

It is not a clinically validated prediction or diagnosis system.

## 2. Source A — Synthetic EHR demonstration data

**Location:** `data/synthetic/ehr_demo.csv`

**Purpose:** Test the data-ingestion and validation pipeline.

**Current fields:** `patient_id`, `age`, `bmi`, `pcos_status`, `data_type`.

**Provenance:** Fictional records generated by the project's own script.

**Limitations:** These records are not collected from real patients and cannot establish medical relationships, clinical risk, or model performance.

## 3. Source B — Synthetic wearable demonstration data

**Location:** `data/synthetic/wearable_demo.csv`

**Purpose:** Test time-series ingestion, timestamp validation, and patient-ID matching.

**Current fields:** `patient_id`, `timestamp`, `heart_rate`, `hrv`, `steps`, `data_type`.

**Provenance:** Fictional records generated by the project's own script.

**Limitations:** The measurements are test fixtures, not real wearable observations or evidence of clinical risk.

## 4. Public dataset candidate — PhysioNet wearable sleep dataset

**Dataset:** A Multi-Night Instantaneous Heart Rate and Accelerometry Dataset with EEG Sleep Stage Labels.

**Official source:** https://physionet.org/content/bidsleep-dataset/1.0.1/

**Potential relevance:** Real wearable heart-rate and accelerometry recordings paired with sleep-stage labels could help explore wearable time-series processing and sleep-related features.

**Access and licence:** The listed version is open access under the Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0. Review the dataset's licence, citation instructions, and data-use conditions before reuse.

**Practical limitation:** The published dataset is large and does not specifically represent people with PCOS. Its sleep-stage labels are not PCOS-related adverse-event labels.

**Status:** Candidate reviewed; not yet downloaded or integrated.

## 5. Data-fusion policy

- Do not join records from independent datasets as if they belong to the same person.
- Do not invent clinical outcomes or present simulated outputs as real predictions.
- Keep synthetic data clearly labelled throughout the pipeline and interface.
- Record each dataset's source, licence, fields, units, missing values, and preprocessing.
- Use real-world data only for tasks its actual labels and study design support.
- If a suitable clinical outcome dataset cannot be established, describe the prototype as a transparent state-estimation and scenario-simulation system rather than a clinically validated risk predictor.

## 6. Current conclusion

The current synthetic files establish that the software pipeline can be tested. The public wearable candidate may support time-series engineering experiments, but it does not by itself validate PCOS-specific risk prediction.

A prediction target must be selected only after the relevant data, labels, and limitations have been reviewed.

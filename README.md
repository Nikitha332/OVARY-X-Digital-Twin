\## Project Title



A Personalized Digital Twin for Predicting Short-Term Metabolic Instability in Women with PCOS



\## Problem Statement



OVARY-X explores the fusion of historical health information and dynamic wearable measurements to estimate evolving metabolic risk.



\## Healthcare Use Case



A research prototype for a clinician-facing virtual patient view and short-term risk estimation.



\## Why Digital Twin?



The system is designed to update a patient-specific virtual state as new observations arrive and support counterfactual simulation.
